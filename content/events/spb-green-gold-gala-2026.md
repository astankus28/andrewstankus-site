Mayfield Village, OH – November 14, 2026 – For one night, [Bellavie](https://bellaviecle.com) became a casino with a cause. The 9th Annual Green and Gold Gala, A Knight at the Casino, filled the ballroom with green velvet, gold sequins and a crowd ready to play for [Saint Paschal Baylon](https://www.saintpaschal.net). The house rules were simple: the odds were always in favor of SPB, and every dollar went toward air conditioning in classrooms across the school, along with other facility improvements.

First-time co-chairs John and Lisa Kane and Greg and Michel Baitt hosted the night and doubled as emcees, picking up where Brent and Cheryl Pietrafese left off after three years at the helm. The evening was presented by The Sandy and Jim Foundation, with Platinum Sponsor [Allergy Immunology Associates](https://www.allergycleveland.com) and Dr. Remie Saab, Premier Sponsor [Seal Tech Asphalt & Concrete](https://www.sealtechcoating.com), Bar Sponsor the Rossi Family, and support from [Bricker Graydon Wyatt](https://www.bricker.com), [Ancora](https://ancora.net) and Mike & Beth Mennel.

The heart of the night belonged to the 2026 Green Knights to Remember. Roberta Modica, whose music has lifted Masses at SPB for years, was honored alongside her late husband Don, who helped begin the endowment that still supports students every year; longtime friend Richard Fratianne presented the award. Then came Mary Connors, who spent 41 years in Catholic education at SPB, 40 of them in junior high, leading Power of the Pen and the Signing Angels along the way. Both emcees were once her students in seventh and eighth grade, and her longest-serving colleague, eighth grade teacher Paul Martin, did the honors.

After grace from [pastor](https://church.saintpaschal.com) Fr. Rosario Pushparaj and a round of raffles drawn by Principal Diane Raguz and Assistant Principal Sheila Klepcyk, [DJ London Bridges](https://www.djlondonbridges.com), Brandon Edmond, took the live auction up a gear: a cooking class and wine pairing with SPB's own Chef Anthony Quagliata, a week on Anna Maria Island, a Disney on Ice suite from [Brennan Industries](https://brennaninc.com), a day on patrol with Officer Jeff and the night's most hotly contested prize, Principal for a Day. Then came Raise the Paddle, every pledge going straight to the classrooms, with the money literally raining down over the checkerboard dance floor. The final $50 round had the room on its feet: the last paddle standing won two Row F seats to the Cavs against the Bucks, with parking and Lexus Lounge access, courtesy of [Lexus of Akron-Canton](https://www.lexusofakron-canton.com).

By 9:00 the silent auction had closed, the dance floor was full and the Green Knights were a little closer to cooler classrooms. Seventy-one years in, the SPB community is still building.

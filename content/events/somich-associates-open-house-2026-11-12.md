Willoughby, OH – November 12, 2026 – Tax season is still months away, but on Thursday the busiest room in Lake County belonged to a CPA firm. Somich & Associates CPAs marked the move into its new office at 38879 Mentor Avenue with a Client & Community Appreciation Open House, and the guest list read like a who's who of the people who keep Northeast Ohio's small businesses running.

From 2 p.m. until well past sunset, clients, colleagues and neighbors drifted through the fresh new space, trading the usual quarterly check-in for drinks, light bites and the kind of conversations that don't happen over email. Owner Andrew Somich and partner David Silverman worked the room like hosts, not accountants: welcoming longtime clients, introducing the team, and showing off a home built for the firm's next chapter.

The verdict? A firm that clearly values its relationships as much as its balance sheets. Here's to the new address, and to the people who made it possible.

La Terraza Nicabanos: Hidden Mediterranean Grill in South Beach

DateNovember 20, 2026
ClientLa Terraza Nicabanos
VenueLa Terraza Nicabanos, 1030 Washington Ave.
CityMiami Beach, FL
CoverageRestaurants & Food
Halal Wagyu, hummus and cigars: we shot the menu at La Terraza Nicabanos, a Mediterranean grill hidden in a 1922 coral rock house in South Beach.

Miami Beach, FL – You've probably walked right past it. On a stretch of Washington Avenue packed with neon and sidewalk hosts, La Terraza Nicabanos sits quietly behind palm fronds in a small house built of coral rock. Owner Akshay will be the first to tell you the location is a challenge: the restaurant isn't on the street the way its neighbors are, so plenty of people stroll by without noticing it. The ones who find it are welcomed with open arms, and they get serious Mediterranean cooking with deep Levantine roots, much of it halal, in a room with real history.

The house dates to 1922, before the Art Deco hotels that made South Beach famous, and its walls are faced in coral rock, the local limestone you hardly see anymore. Local lore says it was built by Henri Levy, the developer behind Normandy Isles, who lived here with his family while building out North Beach.

Start with the Hummus with Meat & Pine Nuts: a wide, silky swirl with spiced beef and toasted pine nuts piled in the center, sitting in warm oil. The Mutabbal is its smoky cousin, charred eggplant whipped with tahini and finished with olive oil and pomegranate seeds. The Steak Wrap is pressed on the griddle until it's striped with char, the Chicken Skewer Bento is an easy full meal over golden rice, and for anyone who just wants a burger, there's a halal Wagyu Burger on a glossy brioche bun with crispy onions. For a bigger night out, the dining room also serves USDA Prime steaks and Japanese A5 Wagyu.

Stay for a cigar. La Terraza runs its own curated cigar program, and Wednesday through Sunday there's live Latin entertainment and DJs from 7 until late. If the hunt isn't your thing, the mezze, wraps and burgers are all on DoorDash. But go in person at least once. A 100-year-old coral rock house with halal Wagyu and a cigar after dinner isn't something you stumble onto every day on Washington Avenue.

Find La Terraza Nicabanos at 1030 Washington Ave., Miami Beach, FL 33139, open Wednesday to Sunday from 4 p.m. until late.

Food and hospitality is the core of what we do. Andrew Stankus Agency is a restaurant and food photographer for restaurants, bars and hotels in Cleveland, across Northeast Ohio and in Miami, working with independent spots and national hospitality brands alike.

We shoot full menus for delivery apps, your website and social, plus the interiors, the bar and the people who make the place work.

What we cover

Full menu shoots. Hero dishes and cocktails. Interiors, patios and ambiance. Chefs, owners and staff portraits. Openings and new-menu launches.

After the night

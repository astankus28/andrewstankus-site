Kind words
Founder

Andrew Stankus

Andrew learned event photography in Miami's busiest rooms, covering nightlife and society events for World Red Eye. Over seventeen years he has photographed galas, brand launches and private parties, and built an advertising practice shooting thousands of campaigns for hundreds of hospitality brands and commercial clients, covering everything those businesses run on: the food, the spaces, the people and the moments that sell them.

Andrew Stankus Agency brings both sides together in Miami and Cleveland: the instincts of an event photographer and the discipline of a commercial one.

— Andrew

Miami throws a party like nowhere else. We've spent seventeen years making sure the pictures keep up.

Andrew Stankus Agency is a Miami event photographer for galas and benefits, corporate events, grand openings, brand activations and private celebrations, across Miami-Dade, Miami Beach and the rest of South Florida.

Andrew has lived and worked in Miami for seventeen years. He learned event photography in the city's busiest rooms, covering more than a thousand events for World Red Eye, from Art Basel and the South Beach Wine & Food Festival to museum dinners at PAMM and galas at the Arsht Center. He knows how a Miami night runs: the late arrivals, the step-and-repeat that has to be right the first time, and the guests who want one great frame and then to be left to enjoy the party.

What we cover

Every kind of event Miami throws: galas & benefits, corporate events, openings & launches, restaurants & food, private celebrations, plus headshots set up on-site.

Where we work

Brickell and Downtown, Wynwood and the Design District, Miami Beach and South Beach, Bal Harbour, Coconut Grove, Coral Gables and Key Biscayne, plus Fort Lauderdale and the rest of South Florida. Hotels and rooftops, museums and galleries, restaurants, private homes and raw spaces turned into venues for one night.

After the night

A select set of highlights first, for social and the press, then the full online gallery. Names are captioned for media, and if you'd like, a public recap goes up on our Events page for sponsors and guests to share.

Video

Questions, answered.

Do you cover events outside Miami?

Yes. We cover all of Miami-Dade and Miami Beach, Fort Lauderdale and the rest of South Florida, and travel for events elsewhere. The agency also works out of Cleveland, Ohio.

What kinds of Miami events do you photograph?

Galas and fundraisers, corporate events and conferences, grand openings and product launches, brand activations, restaurant and hotel events, and private celebrations like milestone birthdays and anniversaries.

Can you send photos to the press during or right after the event?

Yes. We deliver a select set of named, captioned highlights first, formatted for media, social and newsletters, so they go out while the event is still news. The full gallery follows.

Do you offer video and headshots too?

Yes. Photography can be paired with a highlight film or social cuts from our video partner, booked as one team, and we can set up headshots on-site at your event.

How do I check availability?

Send the date, venue and a few details through the form on our homepage, or email andrew@andrewstankus.com. We reply with availability and a tailored quote.

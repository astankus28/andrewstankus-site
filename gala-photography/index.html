CoverageGalas, benefits, awards nights
The room only looks like this once. We make sure the host, the honorees and every sponsor have the pictures to prove it.

Andrew Stankus Agency is a gala and fundraiser photographer for nonprofits, charities and the people who host them, in Cleveland, across Northeast Ohio and in Miami. We cover benefits, charity balls and awards nights the way the society pages do: the arrivals and the step-and-repeat, the room before the doors open, the honorees and the speeches, the paddles going up, and the dance floor once the program is done.

For nonprofits, that means images built for the year ahead: sponsor recognition, donor thank-yous, next year's invitation and the annual report. For the press, it means names captioned correctly and the right people in the frame.

What we cover

Arrivals, red carpet and step-and-repeat portraits. Décor, tablescapes and the room. Honorees, emcees and the program. Live auction, raffles and Raise the Paddle. Sponsors and VIP groups. The after-party.

After the night

A full online gallery for the host, a select set of highlights first for social and press, and a public recap on our Events page that gives sponsors and guests one more reason to share.
